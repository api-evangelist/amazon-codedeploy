re:Invent 2019 is fast approaching and we here at the AWS DevOps blog wanted to take a moment to highlight DevOps focused presentations, share some tips from experienced re:Invent pro’s, and highlight a few sessions that still have availability for pre-registration. We’ve broken down the track into one overarching leadership session and four topic areas: (a) architecture, (b) culture, (c) software delivery/operations, and (d) AWS tools, services, and CLI. In total there will be 145 DevOps track sessions, stretched over 5 days, and divided into four distinct session types: Sessions (34) are one-hour presentations delivered by AWS experts and customer speakers who share their expertise / use cases Workshops (20) are two-hours and fifteen minutes, hands-on sessions where you work in teams to solve problems using AWS services Chalk Talks (41) are interactive white-boarding sessions with a smaller audience.
